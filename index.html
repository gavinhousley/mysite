About

Fullstack JavaScript developer with a background spanning fashion, literature, music and coffee.

Born in Sidcup, Kent, I studied Visual Arts at the University of Plymouth before a detour into bookselling — eventually rising to Head of Fiction at Foyles on Charing Cross Road. Alongside the books came music: singing and playing guitar in the indie trio William and the instrumental Naciente Quartet.

Books and music weren't enough, so I started making my own clothes — a hobby that grew into six years inside the ethical fashion brand Palava, managing production, embroidery and fabric development. Somewhere in there I also picked up linocut printmaking and pulled pints of coffee at Monmouth.

Now I'm retraining as a full-stack developer, merging that artistic sensibility with a love of logic and problem-solving.
